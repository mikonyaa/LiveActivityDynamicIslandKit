# Screenshot and illustration provenance

- `preview-porcelain.png` is a Pillow composition generated by `Tools/render_preview_assets.py`. It is an illustration of the package design, not a system capture.
- `activity-lab.png` and `demo-recipes.png` are historical app screenshots of embedded previews/gallery. Their original capture SHA/device metadata is not retained here; they do not prove current ActivityKit system surfaces.
- New RC captures are exported unedited from named XCTest attachments in the downloadable verification `.xcresult` bundle. App-preview attachments are labelled as app previews. The [stable Home capture](RC-0.1.1/system-home-active-delivery-2.png) shows the simulator's actual compact Dynamic Island (Delivery icon and 8:22) after SpringBoard became foreground and its icons were ready. It uses the production ActivityKit adapter, not an embedded preview. Earlier transition captures are excluded from this set. Metadata and hashes are in the RC publication record.

Embedded previews, hosted bitmap tests and device acceptance serve different purposes. Physical Lock Screen/Dynamic Island and actual VoiceOver acceptance remain pending in [the RC verification record](../../Docs/VERIFICATION-0.1.1.md).
